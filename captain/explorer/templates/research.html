Causal Evidence Story Walkthrough

1. Agent receives task: The user gives the agent a complex goal.
2. Tool called: The agent calls a tool, which produces raw output evidence.
3. Evidence Split: The evidence naturally splits into multiple usage channels (e.g., Channel A goes to reasoning, Channel B goes to a file).
4. Failure Injection: Channel A carries a failure-inducing keyword that confuses the agent.
5. CELA Detection: Our method (CELA) correctly identifies that Channel A is the causal path.
6. Intervention: A channel-level intervention specifically blocks Channel A but preserves Channel B.
7. Success! The failure is prevented, proving causality at the channel level.

What does this research mean?

What happened?

An AI agent used tools to complete tasks. Sometimes, it failed because a specific piece of information (evidence) confused it.

What evidence moved?

Information flows through the system in specific pathways or "channels". We traced exactly where every piece of data went.

What changed?

We tested if we could fix the failures. But instead of just deleting an entire tool's result (which might break other things), we only blocked the specific "channel" that caused the problem.

Why it matters?

This allows us to debug AI agents with surgical precision. Our data proves that "provenance != causality". Just because data came from a bad tool doesn't mean all of its pathways caused the failure. We only label something "causal" when supported by intervention evidence.
