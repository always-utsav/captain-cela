# 06: Benchmark Campaign Summary

The main evaluation campaign executes the benchmark specification at scale to generate statistically rigorous evidence for all tested methods.

- **Scale**: 11 families × 5 seeds × 5 instances per family = 275 distinct evaluation scenarios.
- **Methods Evaluated**: 10 (`B1`-`B5`, `A1`-`A5`).
- **Method Evaluations**: 2750 total method runs.
- **Statistical Comparisons**: 63 comparisons computed per seed to establish hierarchy and ablation significance.

Results validate that the proposed methods (A-series) successfully isolate causal structures defined by the benchmark families over the B-series baselines.
