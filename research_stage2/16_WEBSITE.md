# 16: Website

A companion website is hosted at the `/research` endpoint to visualize data.

**Key Principles:**
- The website is entirely **read-only**.
- It is driven by **frozen data** sets ensuring no dynamic fabrication of results.

**Features/Tabs:**
1. **Story Mode**: Narrative flow of the research.
2. **Granularity Demo**: Interactive visualization of Channel vs. Source-Event interventions.
3. **Results Dashboard**: Detailed benchmark metrics.
4. **Research Mode**: Technical deep dives.
5. **Simple Mode**: High-level, accessible summary.
